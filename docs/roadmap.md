# Outcome Based Roadmap

Dates are intentionally omitted because the portfolio has no staffed delivery team or validated dependencies. The roadmap is sequenced by learning and risk.

## Horizon 1 Make the policy reviewable

**Outcome:** stakeholders can understand and challenge every routing decision.

- Interactive synthetic simulator.
- Visible intent, confidence proxy, factor score, overrides, and rationale.
- Structured handoff card.
- Versioned synthetic eval set and automated policy tests.
- Product, metric, safety, research, and decision documentation.

## Horizon 2 Validate the problem and workflow

**Outcome:** evidence supports the customer problem, taxonomy, and handoff design.

- Customer and specialist research.
- Current-state journey and baseline metrics.
- Annotation guide and adjudication process.
- Approved safety, privacy, compliance, and accessibility requirements.
- Revised risk weights and service-level assumptions.

## Horizon 3 Prove decision quality safely

**Outcome:** routing recommendations perform well enough for specialist-assisted use.

- Approved, de-identified evaluation data.
- Calibrated model or hybrid rules-plus-model approach.
- Slice-level quality and fairness analysis.
- Shadow-mode integration and monitoring.
- Specialist correction and feedback workflow.

## Horizon 4 Improve resolution in a controlled pilot

**Outcome:** eligible cases resolve faster without guardrail degradation.

- Assisted pilot and experiment readout.
- Limited P2 automation.
- Queue-capacity and callback integration.
- Rollback drills and incident process.
- Customer and specialist adoption support.

## Horizon 5 Expand only where evidence supports it

**Outcome:** additional intents or channels meet the same safety and outcome gates.

- Per-slice expansion decisions.
- Voice and multilingual evaluation.
- Drift monitoring and regular policy review.
- Ongoing model, content, and operations governance.

## Dependencies

Real expansion depends on authenticated context, case-management integration, approved data access, queue capacity, model governance, security review, privacy and legal approval, domain specialist participation, and operational ownership.
