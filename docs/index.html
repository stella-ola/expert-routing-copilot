      About this project

      Customers with fraud, billing, or product questions get transferred, repeat themselves, and wait. Expert Routing Copilot is an AI intake layer that works out what the customer needs, how urgent it is, and who should own it, then hands a specialist a summary they can act on without starting over.

      The design rule that matters most: the AI understands, the policy decides. A language model (or, in this baseline, keyword rules) turns a message into structured facts. A deterministic, versioned policy turns those facts into a priority and a route. That keeps the high-stakes decision auditable and testable, and lets the understanding layer improve without changing what "urgent" means.

      This is a product prototype, not a production system. No real customer data, no real accounts, not affiliated with any company.
